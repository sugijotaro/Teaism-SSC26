## About

**Teaism** transforms the ordinary act of drinking — coffee, tea, or water — into a guided, ritualized mindfulness experience rooted in the spirit of the Japanese tea ceremony (茶の湯).

The app was born from watching my younger siblings struggle with the stress of entrance exams, and from my own experience as a Starbucks barista: the realization that a single cup can be a vessel for mindfulness and human connection. Rather than teaching rigid rules, Teaism bridges ancient Zen philosophy and modern life, offering "accessibility of mindfulness" to anyone overwhelmed by a high-stress society.

The entire experience is designed to be completed **within three minutes** — the length of one mindful cup.

## Getting Started

### Requirements

- **Swift Playgrounds 4.6 or later** (iPad / Mac), or **Xcode 16+**
- iOS / iPadOS 16.0+

### Run

1. Clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/sugijotaro/Teaism-SSC26.git
   ```
2. Open `Teaism.swiftpm` in Swift Playgrounds or Xcode.
3. Run the app — **best experienced on an iPad, with sound on and a warm drink in hand.** 🍵
